# 03 — Success criteria for OVATION (written before the build; checked honestly at the end)

Observable by a fresh tester who has never seen the brief, or by the harness. No self-scores.

1. **Feeling in 10 s, hands off.** Without touching anything, a fresh tester can say within 10 s that they are on a stage
   being applauded, and names a feeling (not just "interesting").
2. **The bow is found unaided.** The tester discovers that tapping makes them bow within their first 3 attempts, and
   correctly describes the crowd's response to it without being told.
3. **Discoveries after the first minute.** The tester reports at least two things found after the first minute that
   changed their understanding (e.g. the rhythm emerging on its own, walking off → encore, the house emptying, the last
   clapper). At least one of them is emergent from the model, not scripted.
4. **Muted still lands; sound is true.** A tester given a muted run still describes an emotional moment. With audio,
   the captured spectrogram of a real session shows the roar turning into rhythmic ~2 Hz clapping (envelope periodicity
   strength ≥ 0.5 in that section), a peak roar ≥ 12 dB above the room tone, and no clipping.
5. **Craft on both screens.** Desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844: 0 console errors, ≥ 50 fps desktop and ≥ 40 fps
   phone-emulated in the harness, no horizontal scroll, a first frame within 1 s, works from file:// and as the
   published Artifact, and no tester flags an element as placeholder-looking.
