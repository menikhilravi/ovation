# 02 — The kill round

Five independent critics, each with one lens (prior-art hunter with web search · jaded creative technologist ·
stranger's first 10 seconds on a muted phone · depth & feasibility · emotional truth & shareability).
Each judged all 25 and defaulted to "kill".

## What the critics agreed on

- **Most of the list was "metaphor + pretty shader", or "a paper + a demo".** A citation is not novelty.
- Only three concepts have a verb that means something emotionally: *you receive love* (OVATION), *you ask to belong*
  (WALLFLOWER), *you hear yourself become a stranger* (DRIFT).
- **OVATION**: the highest emotional ceiling, and the only one with a ready-made share sentence ("this website gives you a
  standing ovation"). It dies if (a) it is unearned (a soundboard), (b) the synthesised audio sounds like rain, (c) it
  doesn't land muted, or (d) there's no arc. The second share line has to be *"…and then it takes it away."*
  No synthesised voices; use claps, stomps, whistles, seat creaks and coughs. Pitch-dark apart from spill light.
- **WALLFLOWER**: the most universal raw feeling. Top-down dots read as boids. The body language has to be legible
  (a shoulder turning, a gap closing half a beat late). Rejection must depend legibly on *how* you approach. Late role reversal:
  you open the circle for the next person who arrives alone.
- **FOUNDRY**: the most technically honest, since modal synthesis of an axisymmetric shell is exactly what code can make
  sound real. Its weakness: "tap a bell, it rings" is pleasant, not astonishing. It only lives if the act is direct
  manipulation of the bronze itself, not a profile editor.

## The three to prototype

1. **OVATION**, which I build myself.
2. **WALLFLOWER**, built by an agent from a tight brief.
3. **FOUNDRY**, reframed as *a bell you reshape with your hands while it is still ringing*, built by an agent from a tight brief.
