# 03 — Success criteria (draft, written before the final build; finalised once the pick is made)

Observable, checkable by a fresh tester or the harness. No self-scores.

1. **Feeling without instructions.** A fresh tester who has never seen the brief, with zero text on screen, reports a feeling
   (not just "interesting") and can say what is happening within the first 10 seconds, without touching anything.
2. **The verb is found unaided.** The same tester discovers the core interaction within their first 3 attempts and
   describes its effect correctly without being told.
3. **A second and third discovery.** Testers report at least two things they found *after* the first minute that changed
   how they understood the piece. At least one of them is something the model produced, not something scripted.
4. **Muted still lands.** With audio off, a tester still describes an emotional moment (visual carriers alone).
   With audio on, the captured spectrogram shows the physical behaviour the piece claims (e.g. spontaneous sync).
5. **Craft on both screens.** Desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844: 0 console errors, ≥50 fps on the M3 in the harness,
   no horizontal scroll, no placeholder-looking element in any screenshot, a first frame within 1 s, and nothing that
   needs a settings panel.
